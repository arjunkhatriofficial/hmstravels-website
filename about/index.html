About HMS Travels

Founded in Kolkata to make self-drive car rental simple, transparent and accessible for city errands, airport arrivals and weekend road trips.

Company Story

Built for Kolkata Drivers

HMS Travels began in Kolkata with a clear mission: make self-drive car rental feel as easy as sending a WhatsApp message. We wanted travellers, families and working professionals to get clean, insured cars without waiting for a driver or dealing with unclear rental terms.

Today, HMS Travels serves Kolkata and Howrah with hatchbacks, SUVs and MPVs that suit short city use, airport pickup, family visits and outstation plans. Every booking is handled with practical support, clear communication and a focus on giving customers the freedom to drive on their own schedule.
