Major Pickup Areas

Airport (NSIA)

Kolkata Airport pickup is ideal for flyers who want to continue directly into the city or head toward New Town, Salt Lake or the highway. Share your flight timing and terminal-side landmark so the team can confirm the handover plan.

Howrah Station

Howrah Station pickup works well for train arrivals, family visits and west-side Kolkata plans. HMS Travels can coordinate nearby pickup instructions once your train timing and exit point are clear.

Salt Lake Sector V

Sector V is convenient for offices, hotels and quick access to New Town or EM Bypass. Book a compact SUV or hatchback for business meetings, errands or weekend departures after work.

New Town Action Area 1

Action Area 1 is a practical pickup zone for residential complexes, hotels and airport-side routes. It is especially useful for travellers staying near City Centre 2, Rajarhat and nearby business areas.

New Town Action Area 2

Action Area 2 pickup supports hotel stays, family visits and planned road trips toward the airport corridor. Share your block, tower or landmark for the most accurate handover details.

New Town Action Area 3

Action Area 3 is useful for renters near newer residential zones and highway access points. A self-drive car gives you flexibility for local movement, shopping trips and outstation departures.

Park Street

Park Street pickup is best for central Kolkata stays, business travel and evening plans. Because traffic can be dense, confirm the exact timing and nearby landmark before pickup.

Esplanade

Esplanade works well for central-city access, office errands and trips that begin near major transport routes. HMS Travels can help confirm a practical pickup point that avoids unnecessary traffic confusion.

Dum Dum

Dum Dum is convenient for airport-adjacent travellers and north Kolkata residents. It connects quickly to Baguiati, Lake Town, Salt Lake and the airport road.

Ballygunge

Ballygunge pickup is useful for south Kolkata families, local errands and weekend drives. Choose an MPV for family plans or a compact SUV for a comfortable city and highway mix.

Jadavpur

Jadavpur pickup supports south Kolkata renters heading toward EM Bypass, Garia or central areas. Share your landmark and return timing to confirm availability.

Barasat

Barasat is useful for north-side renters and trips that start outside the densest Kolkata traffic. Confirm your route in advance if you plan an outstation drive.
