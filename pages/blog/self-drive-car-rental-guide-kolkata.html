How to Rent a Self Drive Car in Kolkata

A practical guide to documents, pricing, pickup areas and booking steps for renting a car without a driver in Kolkata.

Rental Guide

Before You Book a Self Drive Car

Renting a self-drive car in Kolkata is useful when you want privacy, flexible timing and control over your route. Instead of waiting for a driver or changing plans around cab availability, you get the keys and drive on your schedule. HMS Travels focuses on Kolkata self-drive rentals with pickup options around Kolkata Airport, Howrah, Salt Lake, New Town, Rajarhat, Park Street, Gariahat and other city areas. You can begin from the HMS Travels homepage, compare vehicles on the fleet page, and send a request through the booking form.

Documents Needed

For most self-drive car rentals in Kolkata, you should keep a valid driving licence ready along with a government ID proof such as Aadhaar or Passport. The person who books the car should usually be the person who drives and presents the documents at pickup. If more than one person may drive, mention that during WhatsApp confirmation so the team can explain what is allowed. Carry original documents or clear digital copies as requested, and make sure your licence is valid for the type of vehicle you plan to rent.

How Pricing Works

Self-drive car rental pricing depends on the model, booking duration, pickup date, return date, pickup area and any special requirements. Compact cars and hatchbacks are generally best for city use and shorter trips. SUVs are useful when you want a higher seating position, more luggage room or a stronger highway feel. MPVs are the practical choice for family travel. Always confirm the per-day rate, security terms, fuel policy, late return rules and any extras before pickup. HMS Travels confirms the final quote on WhatsApp, so you can review the details before committing.

Choosing the Right Pickup Area

Choose a pickup location that reduces your first drive through heavy traffic. Airport pickup works well for travellers landing at NSCBI Airport. Salt Lake and New Town pickups are convenient for office parks, hotels and residential areas near Sector V, Rajarhat and Action Area 1 to 3. Howrah pickup can be useful after train arrival or when your trip begins on the west side of the city. When you fill the booking form, give a clear landmark, preferred handover time and return plan.

Booking on WhatsApp

The quickest route is to choose a car, enter your pickup location, dates, purpose and contact details, then send the request by WhatsApp. A good message includes your name, phone number, preferred car, pickup point, pickup date, return date and whether the trip is local or outstation. HMS Travels reviews availability and replies with confirmation details. Before you arrive, check the car model, documents required, pickup instructions, payment terms and the return process.

Practical Tips for Kolkata Roads

Plan extra time around airport roads, Park Street, Esplanade, Howrah Bridge and office-hour routes toward Salt Lake and New Town. Keep your phone charged for navigation, inspect the car before driving, note the fuel level and take photos during handover if needed. If you are planning an outstation trip, share the route in advance so the team can advise on suitability and terms. Return the car on time, at the agreed fuel level and in the same condition. That simple discipline keeps the rental smooth and avoids avoidable charges.

Final Checklist

Before booking, decide your car type, passenger count, luggage needs, pickup area and trip duration. Keep your licence and ID ready. Confirm pricing, insurance, fuel, return timing and WhatsApp support. If you want a simple place to start, open the HMS Travels fleet, choose a car that fits your route, and submit the booking request. The team will help you complete the next steps and get ready for your self-drive trip in Kolkata.
