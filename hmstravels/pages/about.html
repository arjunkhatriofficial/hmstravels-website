Who We Are

Freedom to Drive

HMS Travels was founded with a simple mission: give every Indian traveller the freedom to drive themselves. No drivers, no middlemen, no drama — just you, the car, and the open road.

We operate across South India's major cities with a modern, well-maintained fleet that ranges from fuel-efficient hatchbacks to spacious SUVs and family MPVs. Every car is comprehensively insured and thoroughly serviced before each booking.

Our booking process is intentionally simple — a WhatsApp message is all it takes. We believe technology should serve you, not complicate things.
